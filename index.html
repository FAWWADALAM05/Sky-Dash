Sky Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Sky Dash mobile game.

1. About Sky Dash

Sky Dash is a mobile game developed for Android devices. The game allows players to fly an airplane, avoid obstacles, collect coins, and achieve high scores.

2. Information Stored by Sky Dash

Sky Dash may store certain game settings and gameplay information locally on your device.

This may include:

  • High score
  • Total coins collected
  • Sound settings
  • Music settings
  • Other gameplay settings required for the operation of the game

This information is used to provide the game's features and is stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising

Sky Dash uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. These advertisements may include rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

For example, the game may offer a rewarded advertisement that allows a player to continue a game after Game Over.

Interstitial advertisements may also be displayed at appropriate points in the game, such as between game sessions.

4. Information Processed by Google Mobile Ads

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process certain information when advertisements are displayed or interacted with. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, device, and applicable privacy settings, this may include:

  • IP address
  • Device and account identifiers
  • Android advertising identifier
  • App or device information
  • Advertising information
  • App interactions, such as ad views and interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Google may use this information for purposes such as advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and improving its advertising services.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Personalized and Non-Personalized Advertising

Depending on your location, device settings, consent choices, and applicable privacy requirements, advertisements may be personalized or may be served without personalization.

Where required, Sky Dash uses Google's consent-management mechanisms to provide users with appropriate privacy choices before applicable personalized advertising is requested.

6. Privacy Choices and Consent

Users in regions where consent is required may be presented with a privacy and consent message before applicable advertising is served.

Depending on the applicable requirements, users may be able to:

  • Give consent
  • Manage privacy choices
  • Decline applicable consent

Privacy choices may affect the types of advertisements that can be displayed.

7. Children's Privacy

Sky Dash is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Sky Dash does not require players to create an account or provide their name, email address, telephone number, or home address in order to play the game.

Advertising services may have their own rules and protections concerning children. Their applicable policies and consent requirements also apply where relevant.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the game. Information transmitted by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is handled according to the security practices of those services.

Google states that data collected by its Google Mobile Ads Unity SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

9. Third-Party Services

Sky Dash uses third-party services to provide advertising functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

The primary advertising service currently used by Sky Dash is:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

10. Data Retention

Gameplay information stored locally by Sky Dash remains on the device until it is cleared by the user, the game is uninstalled, or the game changes or removes the relevant stored data.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is retained according to their respective policies and settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Sky Dash, advertising services, applicable laws, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
